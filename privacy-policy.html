Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Clear by design

Your information should be easy to understand too.

This privacy policy describes the current GoldenGen frontend project. It is a fictional storefront prototype and does not yet process real payments, create accounts, or send contact messages to a production service.

Information we use

The storefront may use information you enter into the contact form, such as your name, email address, subject, and message. At this stage, the form submission is simulated in your browser and is not sent to a backend.

Local storage

To make the prototype useful across pages, your browser may store the following locally on your device:

  • Your shopping cart under goldengen_cart.
  • Your wishlist under goldengen_wishlist.
  • Your product comparison selection under goldengen_comparison.
Clearing the site data for GoldenGen in your browser removes these prototype preferences and cart items. No server-side account or order record is created by this frontend alone.

Contact messages

The current Contact page simulates a successful submission so the interface can demonstrate validation and feedback. Until a backend is connected, GoldenGen does not transmit or retain those messages through this website.

Your choices

You can clear local browser storage and decide what information to enter into the contact form. GoldenGen uses one consistent dark shopping theme. External services such as fonts, icons, or flag imagery may have their own privacy policies when your browser requests those assets.

Future updates

When GoldenGen gains real accounts, inventory, delivery, payments, reviews, or customer support integrations, this page should be updated to explain the actual data flows, retention periods, service providers, and user rights that apply.
